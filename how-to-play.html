Unscripted Physical Laws

Full-Contact Combat & Knockdowns

Valleyball is a high-speed collision sport. There are no fouls, offsides, or penalty whistles. Every interaction is simulated by the Rapier physics engine in deterministic real-time.

Slide Tackle Leg Sweeps

Initiating a slide (RT / C) at speeds exceeding 6.0 m/s transforms your athlete into a sweeping projectile. Connecting with an opponent's legs triggers an immediate ragdoll knockdown, dispossessing them instantly.

Physical Body Bumps

Running directly into an opposing athlete shoves both competitors apart. The faster your closing velocity, the more violent the kinetic impulse. Use shoulder checks to shove defenders away from the goal mouth.

Mutual Slide Clashes

When two athletes slide tackle toward each other simultaneously, both players collide, lose all traction, and enter ragdoll knockdown state together, leaving the ball completely open for secondary athletes.

Heavy Ball Knockouts

A high-velocity spiked ball that strikes an athlete directly in the torso or head carries enough kinetic momentum to knock them off their feet. Time your defensive braces to deflect rather than absorb face-first impacts!

Equipment Specifications

Match Ball Weights & Aerodynamics

The match ball dictates the pace, bounce height, and physical hazard of every rally. Choose your ball diameter during pre-match setup:

Small Striker 0.4m · 0.84 kg

Lightning Reflexes: The lightest and smallest sphere. Highly reactive with extreme aerial velocity and snappy bank rebounds. Punishes missed strikes; rewards twitch timing.

Medium Regulation 1.0m · 2.20 kg

Official Championship Ball: Balanced mass, predictable parabolic flight paths, and optimal sweet-spot response. The standard tournament ball for all competitive play.

Large Exercise Titan 1.5m · 3.89 kg

High-Inertia Juggernaut: Massive surface area, colossal momentum, and heavy collision forces. Shoves athletes backward on contact and easily knocks players down on direct hits.

Tactical Execution

Mastering the Inclined Playing Field

Valleyball is zero-assist physics. There is no magnetizing ball snap or canned animation locks. Your trajectory reads, momentum conservation, and aerial jump timing dictate every rally.

01 // Upward Volleys & Pop Sets

Launch the ball skyward. Pressing Volley (Left Click / E / B) strikes an upward arc. This buys time to reposition, resets defensive shape, and lofts the ball for teammates or self-set aerial spikes.

02 // The Apex Spike Rule

Spike higher = steeper angle. Spiking (Right Click / R / Y) while grounded produces a flat forward drive. Leaping to the peak of your jump and striking the ball at the apex sends a crushing, downward hammer strike directly into the hoop.

03 // Downhill Slide Momentum

Convert gravity into raw speed. Holding Slide (C / RT) while aimed downhill accelerates your athlete down the banked terrain. Use downhill slides to beat rivals to the basin and turn slopes into offensive speed.

04 // Sprinting Against the Incline

Fight gravity up the terrain. Climbing out of the central bowl toward the elevated goal rim rapidly bleeds kinetic energy. Hold Sprint (Shift / LT) along banked lateral tracks to maintain maximum approach velocity.

05 // Committed Ground Dives

High-risk, vital reach. A diving lunge (Q / X) commits your athlete to an unscripted turf skid. Essential for stopping balls on the goal line, but mistiming leaves you grounded while opponents recover the rebound.

06 // Directional Cuts & Plants

Break ankles and redirect. Tapping Cut (V / F / RB) plants your athlete's cleats firmly into the turf, shedding inertia instantly and allowing 90° and 180° direction changes without skidding out of control.

07 // Defensive Brace Shielding

Hold the goal line. Holding Brace (G / LB) lowers your athletic center of mass into a fortified stance. Incoming high-speed spikes bounce off your chest without knocking you backward or causing a stumble.

08 // Instant Goal Inversion

The whistle never blows. The exact millisecond the ball passes through the hoop, targets swap in real-time. The goal you just scored in is instantly yours to defend. Never pause to celebrate—pivot immediately into backcourt defense!
