Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Valleyball is a passion project run by me, Jackson Thomas, in Junction City, Oregon. It is not a company. This policy explains what I collect and what I do with it.

It covers valleyballvs.com, valleyball.org, and the analytics on the Valleyball Versus page on itch.io. Questions are welcome at valleyballdev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • I collect what you enter in my forms, plus analytics about how the sites are used.
  • I use your email for the things you signed up for.
  • I do not sell your personal information.
  • Email me any time and I will show you, correct, or delete what I have.

What you give me

Championship and playtest RSVPs

The RSVP form asks for your email address and what you are coming for: watching, the 1v1 bracket, the 2v2 bracket, or Monday playtests. You can also add a name or gamer tag and a 2v2 partner's name or tag. Please only enter a partner's name or tag they are comfortable sharing.

The Dispatch newsletter

The signup form asks for your email address.

Messages

If you email me, I receive your address and whatever you write.

Form entries go directly from your browser to Google Forms and are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets under the Valleyball Google account.

What is collected automatically

Both sites and the itch.io page use Google Analytics to show me how people find and use them. It records things like pages viewed, scrolling, link clicks, form starts, the site or campaign that sent you, your device and browser type, your language, and your approximate location. When you submit a form, it records that you signed up, which form you used, and for RSVPs which options you picked. It does not receive the email address, name, tag, or partner you type, and I have turned on Analytics' setting that removes email addresses from the page data it collects.

Analytics uses cookies, including _ga and _ga_*, to recognize a browser across visits. They can last up to two years. For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, analytics and ad storage cookies are denied by default so only cookieless aggregate pings are sent. When you follow a link between valleyballvs.com, valleyball.org, and the itch.io page, a _gl value in the link lets Analytics count that as one visitor.

I have also turned on Google signals, which can connect this activity with information from Google accounts of signed-in people who have allowed that connection. I see this only in reports. It does not show me who you are.

I use Google Ads at times to promote Valleyball, and Analytics helps me measure whether those ads lead to visits and RSVPs. I am not currently using this data to build ad audiences or personalize ads.

Other services that load with the pages

  • GitHub Pages hosts the sites and may log technical details such as IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on both sites.
  • cdnjs, run by Cloudflare, supplies a graphics library on valleyball.org.

These services receive your IP address when your browser requests files from them. The game itself is hosted by itch.io, and links to itch.io, YouTube, Twitch, Reddit, and TikTok take you to services with their own privacy policies.

How I use it

  • To plan events, organize brackets, and send you event and playtest details.
  • To send the Dispatch if you joined it.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To understand which pages and promotions are working and to fix problems with the sites.

Events, brackets, and streams

If you play, your name or tag may appear on brackets, on stream, and in posts about the event. The Championship is broadcast live on Twitch and YouTube, and events and playtests may be photographed or filmed. If you would rather not appear in footage I post, tell me at the event or email me and I will do my best to leave you out or take it down.

Who can see it

I keep form responses private. If someone helps run an event, I will share only the bracket or attendance details they need. The service providers named above process data for the purposes described here, and my email is handled by Google. I may disclose information if the law requires it or if it is needed to protect someone's safety.

How long I keep it

  • RSVP details: deleted within 90 days after the December 27, 2026 Championship, so by March 27, 2027.
  • Dispatch emails: kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Google Analytics: user and event data is kept for up to 14 months. Summary reports can last longer.
  • Messages to me: kept while they are useful for running Valleyball, or until you ask me to delete them.

Your choices

  • See, correct, or delete your information: email valleyballdev@gmail.com. I will respond within 30 days.
  • Leave the Dispatch: email me and I will remove you.
  • Limit analytics: block or clear cookies in your browser, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Manage Google ads: use My Ad Center.

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you may have additional privacy rights and may be able to complain to your local data protection authority. Your information is handled in the United States and by service providers that may operate elsewhere.

Age requirements

You must be at least 13 to RSVP or join the Dispatch. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's permission to compete. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted some, email me and I will delete it.

Security

I limit access to the accounts that hold this information and rely on the security of the services above, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
